# Dimension 5: the open scheme has length 4000

**Author:** Yaoling Yang

## Statement and evidence status

Let $X_5=\mathbf P^4\times\mathbf P^4$, $Z_p=b^TD_pa$, $S=Z_0=b^Ta$, and let $Z_5\subset X_5$ be the projective equal-overlap scheme. The repository's $Y_5$ is the open $S\ne0$ scheme. On the characteristic-zero fibre write $U_5=Z_5\cap D(t)=Y_5$, where $Z_pZ_{-p}=t\ne0$ for every nonzero label. The claim is that this open characteristic-zero scheme is finite and reduced of length 4000. It includes non-Hermitian points and is not divided by any symmetry group.

The arithmetic model uses the open $t\ne0$. Modulo 3 its trace satisfies $S^2=6t=0$. Its special open fibre is not the literal open $S\ne0$ over the residue field.

The special-fibre computations, valuation certificates, and algebraic seed records cited here originate in the original 2026 work. The clean replay regenerated and accepted the computational outputs, as recorded in [replay/VALIDATION.md](../replay/VALIDATION.md). This note gives their scheme-theoretic implication. Run `python -B replay/run.py d5` from the repository root.

## 1. The complete open fibre at 3

Over the residue extension used by the saved computation, [e01_fibre.py](../sources/special-fibres/numerics/e01_fibre.py) constructs the full normalized open chart. The saved [e01_d5_p3.out](../sources/special-fibres/numerics/e01_d5_p3.out) records

```text
FULL_DIM 0
FULL_LENGTH 4000
NULL_DIM 0
NULL_LENGTH 2000
TRACE_SQUARE_REMAINDER 0
```

The independent trace-section solve [e03_solve.py](../sources/special-fibres/numerics/e03_solve.py) returns 2000 distinct points and marks the trace-zero chart complete and reduced in [e03_d5_p3_q6561.json](../sources/special-fibres/numerics/e03_d5_p3_q6561.json). The full-fibre analysis [e05_analyze.py](../sources/special-fibres/numerics/e05_analyze.py) verifies `whole_fibre_points=2000`, `whole_fibre_coverage` by Weyl–Heisenberg shifts, and Jacobian ranks `(7,8)` at all 2000 points.

Here is why those shifts cover the open fibre. On $t\ne0$, the equation $Z_{(0,1)}Z_{(0,-1)}=t$ forces $Z_{(0,1)}=\sum_i\omega^ia_ib_i\ne0$. Thus some $a_ib_i\ne0$. The displacement $X^{-i}$ moves that index to zero in both vectors, and separate projective rescaling gives $a_0=b_0=1$. The $X$-translates of this chart therefore cover the whole open fibre. This is the coverage recorded by `whole_fibre_coverage`, with `whole_fibre_points` counting the union.

With $N=b^Ta$, the scheme identity $N^2=6t$ gives $N^2=0$ in characteristic 3. At each point the equal-overlap Jacobian has rank 7 in the eight chart variables, and adding the trace row raises the rank to 8. Thus $N$ gives a nonzero cotangent direction. The maximal ideal is generated by $N$ by Nakayama, and $N^2=0$. The local algebra is $k[N]/(N^2)$ at every point, so the complete open special fibre has scheme length $2000\cdot2=4000$. The nonreduced special fibre supplies the generic length bound.

## 2. Every boundary trait is excluded

Suppose a point of the horizontal closure specializes to $t=0$. The valuative criterion for a finite-type projective closure supplies a trait whose generic point lies in $U_5$ and whose special point lies on the boundary. Normalize its valuation by $v(3)=2$, and write $\mu=v(t)>0$. After separately making the two projective coordinate vectors primitive, Weyl expansion makes every $Z_p$ integral with minimum valuation zero. The equations impose

$$
w_p+w_{-p}=\mu\quad(p\ne0),\qquad
2w_0=v(6)+\mu,
$$

and the zero-weight support is a nonempty set disjoint from its negative. The residue point must also satisfy all rank-one minors, trace, and equal-overlap initial equations. These are necessary conditions for an actual trait, including traits with arbitrary rational weight denominators and residue-field extensions.

The exact support census reduces the realizable boundary supports at $(d,p)=(5,3)$ to 48 supports in two types, represented by masks 992 and 32736 (support sizes 5 and 10). The Weyl and integral Clifford actions used in the reduction preserve the equations and the valuation data. The finite weight-cell certificates exclude both representatives and their group images, and the audit covers the residue-field embeddings in the same support types. A unit identity

$$
1=\sum_i A_i\operatorname{in}_w(f_i)+\sum_j B_jE_j
$$

for a cell proves that no residue solution exists for any weight in that cell. The identities cover all weights admitted by the necessary equations. The final rational linear arithmetic systems are UNSAT for each support type. Thus no boundary trait exists and the schematic closure of the complete generic open scheme has empty intersection with $V(t)$.

The evidence is in [e90_support_audit.py](../sources/escape/numerics/e90_support_audit.py) and its exact arithmetic implementation, the [initial-form audit](../sources/escape/numerics/e90_initial_audit.py), the saved cut/replay files, and [e90_d5_cells_audit.py](../sources/escape/numerics/e90_d5_cells_audit.py). The audit output reports 59 exact polynomial identities, 58 exact small-support cell-formula equivalences, and UNSAT for both support families. In its JSON these appear as `old_minimal_cut_identities=15`, `new_minimal_cut_identities=43`, `large_support_cut_identities=1`, `cut_formula_equivalence_checks=58`, `minimal_final="unsat"`, and `large_final="unsat"`.

Because the horizontal closure avoids $t=0$, its special fibre is a closed subscheme of the finite open special fibre just counted. The closure is torsion-free over the base discrete valuation ring (DVR) by construction, hence flat. Properness and finiteness of the special fibre imply that it is finite. Its generic rank is therefore at most 4000. The no-escape result identifies its generic fibre with $U_{5,\eta}$, giving

$$
\operatorname{length}(U_{5,\eta})\le4000.
$$

This upper bound uses the complete special-fibre scheme length and the trait exclusion. It does not transfer a count of modular points directly to characteristic zero.

## 3. Exact characteristic-zero points and reducedness

The algebraic seed program [e09_exact_d5.py](../sources/exact-d4-d5/numerics/e09_exact_d5.py), its [certificate](../sources/exact-d4-d5/certificates/e09_exact_d5.json), and its [saved output](../sources/exact-d4-d5/numerics/e09_exact_d5.out) verify two exact orbits over a degree-32 number field. The projective extended Clifford group has order 6000. Each seed has exact stabilizer order 3, giving 2000 points per orbit. One seed is Hermitian and the other is a verified non-Hermitian Galois image. Clifford conjugation and transposition preserve Hermiticity, so these orbits are disjoint. They supply 4000 distinct points of the characteristic-zero scheme.

The lower bound equals the scheme-length upper bound. Each of the 4000 distinct points contributes local length at least one, and the total is 4000, so every local ring has length one. This proves reducedness of the original open scheme.
