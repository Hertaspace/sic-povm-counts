# Dimension 4: the open scheme has length 512

**Author:** Yaoling Yang

## Statement and evidence status

Let

$$
X_4=\mathbf P^3\times\mathbf P^3,
\qquad Z_p=b^TD_pa,
$$

with signed integer labels for the displacement matrices, and put $S=Z_0=b^Ta$. Let $t$ denote the common product $Z_pZ_{-p}$, for $p\ne0$, on the equal-overlap locus. Write $Z_4\subset X_4$ for the projective scheme cut out by the complete equal-overlap equations. The repository's $Y_4$ is the open $S\ne0$ scheme. On the characteristic-zero fibre write $U_4=Z_4\cap D(t)=Y_4$. The claim is that this open scheme is finite, reduced, and has length 512 over characteristic zero. It is the full complexified scheme: no Hermiticity condition or symmetry quotient is imposed.

The arithmetic model extends this generic open by $t\ne0$. In characteristic 5, $S^2=5t$ permits $S=0$ even though $t$ is a unit. Its special open fibre is therefore not defined by imposing $S\ne0$ modulo 5.

The finite-field, boundary, and exact-point computations cited below originate in the original 2026 work. The clean replay regenerated and accepted their computational outputs, as recorded in [replay/VALIDATION.md](../replay/VALIDATION.md). This note supplies the length argument. Run `python -B replay/run.py d4` from the repository root to replay the computations.

## 1. The complete open special fibre

Work over a complete unramified discrete valuation ring (DVR) above 5 with residue field $\mathbb F_{625}$, which contains the required displacement phases. The script [e01_d4_fiber.py](../sources/d4-upper/numerics/e01_d4_fiber.py) constructs the full open fibre. Its saved output [e01_d4_fiber.out](../sources/d4-upper/numerics/e01_d4_fiber.out) records

```text
CHART_DIM=0
CHART_LENGTH=512
TRACE_RELATION_REMAINDER=0
TRACE_ZERO_LENGTH=256
TRACE_ZERO_REDUCED=1
A_COMPLEMENT_UNIT=1
B_COMPLEMENT_UNIT=1
```

The two unit-ideal checks certify that the complements of the normalized affine chart are empty after imposing $ty=1$. Thus 512 is the length of the complete open special fibre, not a length on a possibly incomplete chart. The trace-zero quotient has length 256 and is reduced. The equation $N^2=5t$, where $N=b^Ta$, gives $N^2=0$ on this fibre. At each of its 256 support points the quotient by $N$ is the residue field, so the maximal ideal is generated by $N$ and the local length is at most two. Since the total length is $2\cdot256$, every local ring is $k[N]/(N^2)$.

## 2. Boundary length and the intersection budget

Let $\mathcal B_4=Z_4\cap V(t)$ be the projective boundary scheme. The complete primary-multiplicity calculation in [e10_boundary.py](../sources/d4-upper/numerics/e10_boundary.py) covers all 16 disjoint coordinate cells in characteristic zero and in characteristic 5. Its saved outputs [e10_d4_p0.out](../sources/d4-upper/numerics/e10_d4_p0.out) and [e10_d4_p5.out](../sources/d4-upper/numerics/e10_d4_p5.out) each give

```text
SUM_FULL_CELLS=768 COUNTED_CELLS=16
SUM_TRACE_CELLS=384 COUNTED_CELLS=16
SUM_REDUCED_CELLS=48 COUNTED_CELLS=16
```

In particular, the full boundary scheme has length 768 in both fibres. The value 48 is the number of geometric support points after reduction. The boundary is generally nonreduced, and its multiplicity is retained in the length 768.

Each equal-overlap equation is a section of $\mathcal O_{X_4}(2,2)$. If $H_a,H_b$ are the hyperplane classes, six general linear combinations cut a proper zero-dimensional intersection containing $Z_4$. Its Bézout length is

$$
B_4=\int_{\mathbf P^3\times\mathbf P^3}(2H_a+2H_b)^6
   =2^6\binom{6}{3}=1280.
$$

The open special fibre and the quotient boundary contribute at least $512+768=1280$ to $Z_{4,s}$. The Bézout bound therefore forces

$$
\operatorname{length}(Z_{4,s})=1280.
$$

Both parts of the special fibre are zero-dimensional. Upper semicontinuity of fibre dimension then makes every fibre zero-dimensional, and projectivity gives finiteness over the DVR. For a finite module, its generic rank is at most the dimension of its special-fibre reduction. Consequently $\operatorname{length}(Z_{4,\eta})\le1280$. The characteristic-zero boundary computation over $\mathbb Q(\zeta_8)$ gives $\operatorname{length}(\mathcal B_{4,\eta})=768$, hence

$$
\operatorname{length}(Y_{4,\eta})
\le\operatorname{length}(Z_{4,\eta})-768\le512.
$$

This subtraction is the no-escape bound. It counts scheme length, including boundary multiplicity. It does not infer a generic count from a modular geometric-point count.

## 3. The exact lower bound and reducedness

The exact orbit certificate checks a Hermitian algebraic projector and a distinct non-Hermitian Galois image. The projective extended Clifford group has order 1536, and the exact stabilizer of each seed has order 6, so each orbit contains 256 distinct points. Their Hermiticity types make the two orbits disjoint. See [e01_exact_complex_calibration.py](../sources/d4-lower/numerics/e01_exact_complex_calibration.py), its [saved output](../sources/d4-lower/numerics/e01_exact_complex_calibration.out), and the exact orbit checker [e07_exact_d4.py](../sources/exact-d4-d5/numerics/e07_exact_d4.py) with its [certificate](../sources/exact-d4-d5/certificates/e07_exact_d4.json).

The two exact characteristic-zero orbits give 512 distinct points of $Y_{4,\eta}$. Together with the upper bound, they force $\operatorname{length}(Y_{4,\eta})=512$. A finite scheme of length 512 containing 512 distinct geometric points has local length one at every point. Thus $Y_{4,\eta}$ is reduced.

The same equality makes the total projective family and its boundary flat: their generic and special lengths are respectively 1280 and 768. On the special fibre the open part and boundary exhaust all length, so the corresponding idempotent splitting lifts over the DVR. The open part is therefore a finite flat component of rank 512. No horizontal branch enters through $t=0$. As a separate cross-check, the exact calibration transcript records a direct characteristic-zero rational Gröbner quotient of dimension 512.
